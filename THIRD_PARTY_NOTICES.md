The application downloads and can bundle snapshots of the AdGuard Base, Tracking Protection, Mobile Ads, and Japanese filters from AdGuard's official HTTPS endpoints. Filter content remains subject to its upstream licenses and terms. Source URLs are displayed in the application’s ad-block settings page.
